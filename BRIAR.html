Origami Crane

I have a habit of folding a lot of origami cranes. Sometimes the impulse comes during long, tedious lectures, or before particularly nerve wracking events, and at other times it strikes completely out of nowhere. I first started making these cranes with the goal of collecting 1000 of them, though I’ve stopped keeping count since the 400 mark. Now, it's just a habit that feels natural to me; I’ve repeated the process enough times that I am confident in folding cranes with my eyes closed. I keep these paper cranes in a designated container, or sometimes in miscellaneous locations that I access often— in a corner of my bag, my pockets, or a desktop container. I often end up forgetting about them, so it becomes a nice little surprise when I reach into my bag and find a handful of cranes. In a sense, these cranes function as a lucky charm for me. The folding process itself brings me comfort, and whenever I see one of these colorful cranes, I am reminded that nothing is ever so serious.
